About AnimesFreak

AnimesFreak is a fast, clean place to discover anime series and movies with multi-audio options — Hindi, English, Tamil, Telugu and more — in 480p, 720p and 1080p.

We are an index, not a host: every link points to third-party sources already available online. New titles are added regularly, and you can always request one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is AnimesFreak free?

Yes. Streaming and downloading are free.

A video won't play — what should I do?

Try another quality (480p is the most compatible on phones). If it still fails, use Report Link Not Working.

How do I download an episode?

Open the episode, tap Download under the player and choose a quality.

Can I request a title?

Absolutely — use the Request page.

Do you host the files?

No. We only index third-party links. See DMCA.

Privacy Policy

We collect only what you give us: the email and message you submit in comment, report, request, DMCA and contact forms, used solely to respond and moderate.

Your favourites, watch history, theme and likes are stored in your own browser (localStorage) and are never sent to us.

Third-party hosts and ad/short-link services we link to have their own policies. We do not sell personal data.

Terms of Service

By using AnimesFreak you agree to use it for personal, non-commercial purposes only. We provide an index of third-party links and make no guarantee of availability or accuracy.

Do not post unlawful, abusive or spam content in comments. We may remove comments and links at any time.
